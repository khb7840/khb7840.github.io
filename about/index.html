About

Hyunbin Kim received his PhD in 2025 and is currently a postdoctoral researcher in SteineggerLab at Seoul National University, South Korea. His expertise lies in massive protein structure data processing and developing scalable bioinformatics software, alongside bioinformatics, cancer genomics, protein structure analysis, and data visualization. He is the developer and maintainer of FIREVAT, Foldcomp and Folddisco.
